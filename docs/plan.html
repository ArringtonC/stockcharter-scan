The scan runs itself at 9:15am and 5:15pm ET. You do not need to watch it, and watching it is how you end up taking a trade that did not fire.

Nine days in ten nothing fires. That is the design, not a fault.

Month end — the five questions

Deposit in? Every trade had a setup? Every call had 45+ days? Nothing over the cap? Account above the floor line?

Five yeses means the plan is working, even in a month you lost money. That is the point of having rules instead of results.

The one rule

If it did not fire, it is not a trade.

Both real trades broke this. PYPL had no setup behind it and won anyway, which is worse than losing — it taught the wrong lesson. BABA was an 18-day call when the rule says 45 minimum, and it cost $82.

Net on real money so far: −$32 across two trades, both of which broke a rule you had already written.

Why $30,000 and not $90,000

One number changes what you are allowed to buy. The other is just further away.

At $30,000 a LEAPS contract is 10% of the account instead of 35%. That is the trade with an 87% win rate at 730 days — the strongest result in this project, and the one your cap currently locks you out of.

Nothing changes shape before $30,000. Everything changes shape at it.

What would make me wrong

A tested edge that survives the bar. Fifty ideas failed this year. The fifty-first might not. That changes the return line, not the floor line.

A bigger deposit. You said you can do more. Every extra dollar moves the date forward further than any strategy found so far — but hold it as the repair tool until you need it.

Buying the delisted-securities data. $500 once. Every backtest here is inflated by an unknown amount because the companies that died are not in the sample. Until that is fixed, the 40% line is optimistic and the floor line is not.
