How $8,500 becomes $3,000 a month.

Not by trading better. By trading the same and getting bigger. The arithmetic below is the whole argument, and it says the account is the constraint — not the strategy.

The arithmetic first

This is the part that settles it. Everything after is a consequence.

account today$8,50018 Sep 2026
target$36,000$3,000 a month
return that needs424%every year, forever

For scale: Buffett's lifetime average is about 20% a year. The best result anything in this project ever produced was under 100%, and it did not survive having its five biggest contributors removed.

424% a year is not a target. It is a way to lose the account. Anything sized to reach it has to be sized to lose everything, and on an option that happens in one week.

Where the real money is, and why you cannot reach it

The strongest result in this entire project is LEAPS on Setup F — an 87% win rate at 730 days.

You cannot trade it. One contract costs about $3,000 and your cap is $600.

That is the finding. The edge exists and the account cannot reach it. Everything below is about closing that gap.

Why both halves are needed

Deposits alone and returns alone each take seven years. Together they take three.

Both togetherNov 2029 3 years 2 months. Less than half the time of either alone.

Read the second row twice. A 40% annual return on $8,500 is worth less than $1,000 a month of deposits. Deposits are also the only part fully under your control.

What we do

1

Change the target to $500–$800 a month

That is what $8,500 can support. Hitting it consistently for six months is the evidence that size is worth adding. Aiming at $3,000 today means sizing for it, and sizing for it is how the account ends.

2

Deposit $1,000 a month, on a date, automatically

It beats every edge we found this year. It is the only lever that cannot fail.

The table above: deposits alone reach the target faster than a 40% annual return alone.

3

Follow the rules you already wrote

Both real trades broke them. PYPL had no setup behind it. BABA was 18 days when the rule says 45 minimum, and the stop was 1.38% against a stock that moves 1.40% on a median day.

The BABA post-mortem says a 5% stop was never touched and returned +$220 on the same entry. Following the existing rules costs nothing and was worth $300 on one trade.

4

Keep testing, and expect it to fail

About fifty ideas were tested in one session. Nearly all failed — VWAP, equal price, touch reversal, sector rotation, the weekly cross, earnings sentiment.

That is the process working. A failed test costs an afternoon. A failed live trade costs $600.

5

Revisit at $30,000

That is where the 87% LEAPS cell becomes reachable and the shape of the plan changes. Do not re-plan before then. The numbers will not have moved enough to mean anything.

The monthly check

Five questions, once a month. If the first three are yes, the plan is working.

Did the $1,000 deposit go in?
Did every trade taken have a setup behind it?
Did every call have 45 days or more at entry?
Did any position exceed $600, or any name exceed 20% of the account?
Is the account on or above the line in the path table?

What would make me wrong

Three things, and only these three:

A tested edge that survives the bar. Fifty ideas failed. The fifty-first might not. That changes the return assumption, not the arithmetic.

More capital than $1,000 a month. Every extra dollar moves the date forward more than any strategy we have found.

Buying the delisted-securities data. $500 once. Every backtest here is inflated by an unknown amount because the companies that died are not in the sample. Until that is fixed, every number on this page is optimistic.
