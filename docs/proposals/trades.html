Kept

  • Every number. Entry, mark, P/L, target, DTE, strike, expiry, contracts, sessions held — all still on the page, none recalculated.
  • The mark source. alpaca / schwab versus iv / est is the difference between a quote and a model, and it stays visible.
  • REAL treatment. Real-money rows keep the red tag and now sort to the top.
  • Open and closed stay separate, and closed still splits win from loss.
  • The approved Today grammar — same tokens, same rows, same tags, same disclosure pattern.

Changed

  • A ten-column table became a row you can read. Symbol · what you hold in plain words · P/L · progress. Everything else expands.
  • The mechanics fold away. Entry, mark, strike, expiry, DTE, contracts, sessions and cost basis live behind the row, not across it.
  • The rule that applies is on the row. "Floor set at $126.71" or "No stop. Target is the prior high" — read from the existing setup and status fields, not invented.
  • Performance carries its sample size. "67% win rate" over 3 closed trades now prints the 3. A win rate on a handful of trades is not a win rate.
  • Setup E is marked retired where it appears, instead of looking like a live setup.
  • Phone gets rows, not a sideways scroll.

One judgement call to check

Seven open positions carry setup E, which was retired. On the current page they look identical to live setups. I tagged them E · retired in grey rather than hiding them, because they are real positions and the record should show what they were opened under. Tell me if you would rather they read differently.
