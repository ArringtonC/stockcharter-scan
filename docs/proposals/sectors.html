Kept

  • The leadership board — all eleven sectors, ranked, with the ten-week rank line.
  • Expandable holdings, with your own names marked inside each sector.
  • "Context, not signal" stays the governing idea and is now stated in the first line.
  • Every number: rank, weekly and monthly rank change, relative strength, gap to the 200-day, churn.

Changed

  • The refresh control is gone. It belongs to System, page 8.
  • Rank moved to the left edge and is the only thing sized like a number that matters.
  • The rank line got a scale. It is inverted so up means better, and the final point is marked — before, it was a sparkline with no anchor.
  • The failed test is stated up front. Rotation failed as a strategy and as a filter, and the page now says so before you read the board, not after.
  • Holdings became a two-column list with your names in amber, instead of a paragraph of tickers.
  • Sectors with none of your names say so, rather than showing an empty space.

One judgement call to check

The amber accent appears twice on this page: on the top three ranks, and on your own names inside a sector. Everywhere else the page is grey. That is deliberate — a leadership board where everything is coloured reads as eleven recommendations. If you want the relative-strength column coloured green and red throughout, say so, but I think it makes a context page look like a signal page.
