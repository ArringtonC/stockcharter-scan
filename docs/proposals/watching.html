Kept

  • The four-way distinction. Tested signal, watchlist-only research, blocked candidate and market context stay four different things and are labelled as four different things.
  • Every condition and every number — drawdown, RSI, the 10/30 gap, the F band, sector rank, holdings, relative strength.
  • Why each one is not tradeable, in its own words: Setup D never beat random picking, the weekly cross is +0.49pp at p=0.363.
  • Sector leadership stays "context, not signal" with expandable holdings.

Changed

  • One home. Four locations across two tabs become one page.
  • Status is the organising idea, not setup name. Near · Blocked · Weekly, in that order, because that is the order of how close a thing is.
  • Every row names its next condition — what has to happen, or why nothing can.
  • Nothing is styled like a signal. No amber, no green, no progress bar, no ranking. The accent appears once, on your own names inside a sector.
  • Sector context is filtered to your names first. Eleven sectors is a research page; which of your 63 names sit in the leading sector is the part that belongs here.
  • Blocked says what blocked it — "trend broken" and "no SEC data" were already in the data and were never shown as the headline.

Two judgement calls to check

1. Order. I sorted Near → Blocked → Weekly by how close each is to being tradeable. Blocked sits above Weekly because a blocked name already passed the price test and failed one condition, where a weekly cross passed a test that did not clear the bar. Say if you read that the other way round.

2. Sectors. The full eleven-sector board is research and I think it belongs on the Sectors page, not here. Watching shows the leading sector expanded with your names marked, and the rest collapsed. If you want the whole board here instead, that is one line.
