What this is. Two simulated $100,000 accounts run by rules, every trading day, with no hands on them. Every idle dollar sits in SPY. A Setup F fire at the close becomes an order; the order fills at the first run of the next session at the live price, paid for by selling SPY; 0.10% each way. F V1 sells after 252 sessions. Shadow C sells at the next open after the stock has touched its target and then closed under its 30-day EMA. Dividends are reinvested, splits adjust the holding. If the book cannot pay for a fire, the opportunity is written down as unfunded and nothing is sold to make room. Every run replays the order ledger from $100,000 and must land on the book's state. Paper only: no broker, no real money, no order ever leaves this page. Shadow C is a test of an exit rule, not a signal.

Still by hand: the Schwab account itself, deposits, and any real order. Pause or stop the manager from the box with portfolio_manager.py pause | resume | kill.
